plantstream: an unkillable plant cam broadcast

Engagement type
Streaming appliance build
Platform
Debian VM · Docker · FFmpeg

An IP camera pointed at houseplants, a lofi soundtrack, and a virtual machine that never asks for attention: plantstream composites the camera feed in a headless browser, encodes it at broadcast-steady constant bitrate, and streams to YouTube around the clock — detecting and healing its own failures, because the only thing slower than the plants should be the pager.

The challenge

  • The camera page stalls silently — the video element freezes to black while everything else looks healthy, and nobody is watching a plant cam closely enough to notice.
  • Nearly-static content makes encoders undershoot bitrate, tripping platform ingest warnings and stalling stream startup.
  • Headless box, no display, no desktop — yet the feed needs a real browser to render, and everything must survive reboots.

The solution

  • Xvfb + Chromium in kiosk mode render the camera page on a virtual display; FFmpeg captures it with x11grab and mixes a looping music playlist.
  • A luma-sampling watchdog crops the camera region once a minute; consecutive dark frames trigger a browser reload and the stream self-heals mid-broadcast.
  • True CBR encoding (VBV + nal-hrd) pads the motionless scene to a constant 6800 Kbps, so ingest sees a rock-steady feed regardless of how little the plants do.

How the broadcast runs

1 · CAPTUREChromium renders the cam page on a headless virtual display
2 · WATCHWatchdog samples frame luma every 60s; black feed → reload
3 · ENCODE1080p30 CBR video + looping lofi playlist, mixed in FFmpeg
4 · BROADCASTRTMP to YouTube; the encoder restarts itself forever

Engineering highlights

  • Black-frame detection built from stock FFmpeg — signalstats luma averaging over a cropped sample box, no extra dependencies.
  • The critical encoder runs as the container's main process under restart-unless-stopped: one supervision tree, no orphans.
  • Stale X locks and orphaned processes cleaned on every boot, so crash loops can't wedge the display.
  • All tunables (check cadence, darkness threshold, sample crop) are env config — field-adjustable without an image rebuild.

Solved during the build

  • Platform exit — removed a second streaming destination cleanly; the surviving encoder gained its freed CPU.
  • Ingest warm-up stalls — "preparing stream" limbo traced to bitrate undershoot on static scenes; fixed with padded CBR.
  • Silent camera death — the stream now notices its own black feed and recovers without a human ever seeing it.
  • Reboot fragility — display locks, process cleanup, and container restart policy make power loss a non-event.
